BECOME A LEGEND

Apex Legends is a fast-paced battle royale game where players form squads using characters called Legends, such as Bloodhound, Lifeline, and Pathfinder. Each Legend has unique tactical skills, passive abilities, and ultimate moves that help the team win. Players drop into diverse maps like King's Canyon, Olympus, and World's Edge to search for weapons, armor, and loot. Across different game modes, squads must use smooth mobility mechanics including sliding, ziplining, and a ping system for quick communication to outsmart enemy teams and be the last squad standing. Apex Legends is available on PC, PlayStation, Xbox, and Nintendo Switch.

Target Audience: Competitive gamer’s aged 13 to 30+
