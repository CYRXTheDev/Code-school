GAME MECHANICS:

Core Character and Team Mechanics:

Legend Classes

Pick a character with a unique Passive, Tactical, and Ultimate ability that dictates team synergy (such as healing, scouting, or defense).

Banner Respawns

If a teammate dies, pick up their Death Banner and bring them back to life at designated Respawn Beacons.

The Ping System

Communicate enemy positions, loot, and movement entirely non-verbally with a context-sensitive ping button.

GAME MECHANICS:

Movement Mechanics

No Fall Damage

Fall from any height without losing health (though extreme drops trigger a brief landing slow/stun unless animation-canceled).

Advance Momentum

Utilize wall jumps, zipline super-jumps, air-strafing, and tap-strafing to outmaneuver opponents in gunfights.

Slide-Jumping

The foundational movement trick; sprint, crouch to slide down a slope or flat ground, and immediately jump to preserve high velocity.

GAME MECHANICS:

Combat and Survival Systems:

Shield Batteries & Cells

Use consumable healing items mid-fight to recharge depleted shields, or syringes and med-kits for raw health flesh damage.

Evolved Shields

Equip body shields (White, Blue, Purple/Gold, Red) that expand your health pool and upgrade by dealing damage to enemies.

Weapon Looting

Match 8 distinct weapon classes with correct ammo types (Light, Heavy, Energy, Shotgun, Sniper, Arrows, and Supply Drop red weapons).
