GAME MODE:

Match Making

Ranked Battle Royale

Ranked Battle Royale follows the same rules as Battle Royale but adds a competitive ranking system.

Battle Royale(Trios & Duos)

Battle Royale is the main game mode in Apex Legends. Up to 60 players are divided into 20 squads of 3 (Trios) or 30 squads of 2 (Duos) and compete to become the last squad alive.

Team Deatmatch

Drop into a 6 vs. 6 match where points are scored by killing your opponents. First team to 50 points takes the match!

Control

Join 9 vs. 9 battles and team up to capture and hold control points on the map, with infinite respawns to keep the action going.

Gun Run

Face off against three other squads and land kills to ascend through a track of 25 weapons.

Firing Range

The Firing Range is a practice area where players can improve their skills.
